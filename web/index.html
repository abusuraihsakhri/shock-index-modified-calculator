Example

Shock Index SI

— HR ÷ SBP

Modified Shock Index MSI

— HR ÷ MAP

Mean Arterial Pressure MAP

— DBP + (SBP − DBP) ÷ 3 · mmHg

Age Shock Index Age-SI

— Age (years) × SI

Reverse Shock Index RSI

— SBP ÷ HR

Values are rounded for display; underlying calculations use full precision. MSI research intervals differ from SI intervals.

Interpretation and limitations

SI and MSI associations depend on setting, population, measurement conditions and confounders (e.g., medication effects on heart rate). Historical SI cutoffs of 0.7 and 0.9 are descriptive, not universal rules. In observational emergency department studies, MSI values outside approximately 0.7–1.3 were associated with adverse outcomes. These values do not establish diagnosis or management.

Age-SI is age × SI, not SI divided by normalized age. Adult intervals should not be extrapolated directly to children; no pediatric risk stratification is provided by this browser tool.

Sources: Zarzaur et al., 2008 · Liu et al., 2012 · Al Jalbout et al., 2023
